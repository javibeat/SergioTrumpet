The Signature Sound

Sergio is not just a DJ; he is a musical architect bridging the gap between classical acoustic elegance and modern electronic energy.

With years of classical training on the trumpet and an inherent passion for deep house, techno, and electronic rhythms, Sergio crafts unforgettable auditory experiences. From intimate ibiza-style beach clubs to massive festival stages around Europe, his sets are a journey.

When the trumpet sounds over a driving bassline, the crowd connects on a deeper level. It’s a live performance that demands to be felt.
